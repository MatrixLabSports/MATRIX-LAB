# MATRIX V18 — Data Inventory & Compliance Evidence Governance

V18 introduces a neutral, evidence-based governance layer for:

- data inventory;
- purpose limitation;
- jurisdiction review evidence;
- cross-jurisdiction transfer review evidence;
- a fail-closed compliance evidence gate.

## Non-goals

V18 does **not** determine legal bases by itself, does **not** declare a jurisdiction legally adequate, and does **not** self-certify compliance with Colombia, GDPR, CCPA, or any other legal regime. Legal conclusions remain external/human-reviewed evidence.

The maximum software outcome is that evidence is complete enough to be eligible for independent legal review.
