# Compliance Evidence Policy V18

1. Every governed data asset must appear in a versioned inventory.
2. Personal-data assets must identify data-subject categories and an internal privacy-review reference.
3. Every processing operation must match a pre-registered purpose and data-category scope.
4. Every jurisdiction in which an asset is governed must have current external review evidence for the exact purpose.
5. Cross-jurisdiction transfers require route-specific evidence unless source and destination are the same jurisdiction.
6. Missing, future-dated, expired, revoked, mismatched, or rejected evidence fails closed.
7. `REVIEW_REQUIRED` produces WATCH, never PASS.
8. Software evidence gates may determine readiness for legal review but may not certify legal compliance.
9. Compliance gates may not promote models or enable wagering.
