# MATRIX Data Protection Policy — V16 Draft Technical Contract

1. Collect only fields required for an explicitly documented purpose.
2. Personal data must never be classified below CONFIDENTIAL.
3. Credential-bearing artifacts must be RESTRICTED.
4. CONFIDENTIAL/RESTRICTED data requires current evidence of encryption at rest and in transit and externally managed keys.
5. Every governed asset requires an assigned retention policy.
6. Legal hold overrides automatic deletion.
7. A completed deletion must preserve evidence of what was deleted without preserving the deleted payload itself.
8. CONFIDENTIAL/RESTRICTED exports are never automatically allowed; they require review evidence.
9. Backups are part of the protection boundary and require explicit protection evidence.
10. Privacy/data-protection status may block or downgrade readiness, but cannot promote a model or enable automatic wagering.
11. This technical policy is not a claim of compliance with any law or regulation. Jurisdiction-specific legal review is separate.
