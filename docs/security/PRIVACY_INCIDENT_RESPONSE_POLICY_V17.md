# PRIVACY INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY — V17

V17 distinguishes **technical incident detection** from **legal breach determination**.

Technical states:

`DETECTED → TRIAGED → CONTAINED → REMEDIATED → CLOSED`

Technical severity levels are `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, and `CRITICAL`. Severity is based on data classification, personal-data involvement, confirmed external exposure, integrity compromise, and credential exposure.

Closure requirements:

- root cause and remediation reference are mandatory;
- HIGH/CRITICAL incidents require independent review;
- when legal assessment is required, it must be completed before closure;
- an incident cannot close while a required notification remains incomplete;
- legal notification is never sent automatically by this subsystem.

The legal status fields are evidence gates only. They do not claim compliance with Colombia, GDPR, CCPA, or any other jurisdiction.
